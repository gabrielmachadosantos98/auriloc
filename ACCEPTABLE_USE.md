# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft.** This policy has not been reviewed by a lawyer yet. It states the intent of
> the project and will be revised before any commercial release.

Auriloc simulates the GPS location reported by an iPhone that you own or are authorized
to use. It is built for **software development, testing, QA and product demonstrations**.

## Intended uses

- Developing and testing apps that use location (maps, delivery, mobility, logistics,
  fitness, geofencing, location-based notifications).
- Reproducing location-dependent bugs and running repeatable location scenarios.
- Demonstrating location features of your own product.

## Prohibited uses

You must not use Auriloc to:

- Deceive any person or organization about where you are in order to obtain money,
  services, benefits or advantages you would not otherwise get. This includes faking
  attendance (time clocks, gym or class check-ins), delivery or ride-hailing
  activity, insurance, banking or any other fraud.
- Break the terms of service of third-party apps or services.
- Interfere with law-enforcement, safety, emergency or monitoring systems.
- Simulate the location of a device you do not own or are not authorized to control.

## Detection

Since iOS 15, apps can detect that a location is being simulated by software. Third-party
apps may flag, limit or ban accounts that use simulated locations. Auriloc does not and
will not try to hide simulation from apps.
