Privacy notice

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This notice explains what data the Auriloc website collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers this website and the waitlist only, not the Auriloc software, which runs on your computer and does not send us any data.

What we collect

  • Waitlist answers: your email address and the answers you choose to give in the form (such as your role, the kind of app you work on and how you test location today).
  • Anonymous visit statistics: page views, referring sites and country, collected without cookies and without identifying you.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you about early access to Auriloc.
  • To understand who needs Auriloc and decide what to build next.

We process waitlist data based on your consent, given when you submit the form. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Tally (tally.so) stores the waitlist form answers.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and provides cookie-free visit statistics.

These providers may store data outside your country, under their own data protection commitments.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist answers until Auriloc's public launch, plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and similar laws, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email gabrielmachadosantos98@gmail.com and we'll reply within 15 days.
